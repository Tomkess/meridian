Yield" with ticks from -1750 to 0 and a small positive tail to roughly +60; the Y axis is labelled
"Avg FCF Yield" with ticks from -2k to 4k. Nine points are plotted: six sit almost on top of each
other at approximately (0, 0), and four are spread far out at roughly (-1600, -1250), (-320, +3400),
(-165, -200), and (+60, +400). Neither axis is bounded the way a percentage yield would be — the
ranges run to four digits in both directions, so these values are not percentages. Both axis labels
also carry an "Avg" prefix, meaning a ratio is being averaged across entities rather than aggregated
across them, which are different numbers. Six points at the origin alongside four extremes means the
distortion is confined to a handful of entities rather than applying uniformly. A table-view toggle
and a kebab menu are visible in the top-right corner, so this is a rendered insight, not an editor view.
